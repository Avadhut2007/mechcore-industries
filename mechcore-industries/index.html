Built around one job: make the part match the drawing.

Mech‑Core Industries is a precision engineering shop working across CNC and VMC machining, jigs and fixtures, sheet metal, transmission components and heavy fabrication. Customers bring us a drawing, a worn sample to reverse-engineer, or a rough sketch of what a part needs to do — we bring the machines, the gauges and the process discipline to get it right, repeatably.

Every job runs through the same checks: dimensional verification against the drawing, surface and fit inspection, and a documented first-piece approval before a batch is cut. Nothing ships on a guess.

Vision

To be a leading name in precision-engineered mechanical parts — known as much for consistency as for capability, and trusted to get demanding specifications right the first time.

Mission

Deliver high-quality, precision components that meet each customer's exact specification, backed by careful craftsmanship, honest timelines and a genuine partnership approach.

What we machine, cut and build

Seven capability areas, one shop floor — parts move between processes without ever leaving the building.

CNC Machined Components

Turned prototypes and production runs of shafts, bushes, flanges and fittings, held to tight dimensional tolerance.

VMC Machined Components

3-axis milled housings, brackets and splined shafts — intricate geometry cut straight from solid billet.

Jigs & Fixtures

Machining, welding and assembly fixtures built in-house to hold your parts exactly where they need to be, every cycle.

Laser Cutting & Bending

Clean-edge laser cutting and precision bending for tube and sheet — brackets, enclosures and formed assemblies.

Transmission Components

Sprockets, gears, pinions, racks and chain components machined to run true under load.

Conveyor Rollers

Rollers and roller tables manufactured for material-handling lines, built for continuous duty.

Fabrication Job Work

Heavy fabrication and welded sub-assemblies, plus special purpose shafts and flanges made to order.
